# Lumen Analytics Employee Handbook

## Working hours and remote work
- Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday through Friday.
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without manager approval.
- Working remotely more than 3 days per week requires written approval from the department head.
- Employees working from another country must notify HR at least 30 days in advance and may do so for at most 20 days per calendar year.

## Leave
- Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- Sick leave is 12 days per year and a medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of absence.
- Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- Employees receive 2 paid volunteering days per year.
- Bereavement leave is 5 days for immediate family and 2 days for extended family.

## Expenses and equipment
- Business expenses must be submitted in the Expensio tool within 30 days of purchase.
- Expenses above 250 euros require pre-approval from a manager.
- Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day.
- Employees receive a laptop refresh every 36 months.
- The home office stipend is 400 euros, paid once, after the first 90 days of employment.
- Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy class.

## Learning and performance
- Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 euros.
- Conference attendance beyond the learning budget needs approval from the VP of the department.
- Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December.
- Ratings use a five-point scale where 3 means meets expectations.
- Promotion proposals must be submitted by managers no later than 15 May for the summer cycle.

## Onboarding and employment terms
- New hires complete security awareness training within their first 7 days.
- The probation period lasts 6 months and the notice period during probation is 2 weeks.
- After probation, the standard notice period is 2 months.
- Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 60 days.
- Company equity is granted through a stock option plan, with a 4-year vesting schedule and a 1-year cliff.

# Lumen Insights Product Guide

## Plans and pricing
- Lumen Insights offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise.
- The Starter plan costs 49 euros per month and includes 3 user seats.
- The Growth plan costs 199 euros per month and includes 15 user seats.
- The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and includes unlimited user seats and single sign-on.
- Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount compared to monthly billing.
- A 14-day free trial is available for the Starter and Growth plans without a credit card.

## Usage limits
- The Starter plan allows up to 10,000 events per day.
- The Growth plan allows up to 500,000 events per day.
- API rate limits are 60 requests per minute for Starter, 600 requests per minute for Growth, and 3,000 requests per minute for Enterprise.
- The maximum size of a single CSV import is 200 MB.
- Dashboards can contain at most 30 widgets each.
- Exported reports are limited to 100,000 rows per export on the Starter plan.

## Data retention
- Starter plan data is retained for 90 days.
- Growth plan data is retained for 13 months.
- Enterprise customers can configure retention up to 7 years.
- Deleted workspaces are permanently purged after 30 days.

## Integrations
- Lumen Insights integrates natively with Slack, Salesforce, and BigQuery.
- The BigQuery connector syncs data every 15 minutes on the Growth plan and every 5 minutes on the Enterprise plan.
- Webhooks are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans only.
- The Slack integration can send scheduled report digests at daily or weekly frequency.

## Support and service levels
- Starter customers get email support with a response target of 2 business days.
- Growth customers get email and chat support with a response target of 8 business hours.
- Enterprise customers get a dedicated account manager and a 1-hour response target for critical incidents.
- The platform uptime SLA is 99.9 percent for Growth and 99.95 percent for Enterprise.
- Support is available Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time.
- Service credits of 10 percent of the monthly fee are issued when uptime falls below the SLA.
